# Enclosure Workspace

This directory reserves the future mechanical work stream for the source CAD,
exported STEP, printable 3MF/STL, bill of materials, and assembly notes. Do not
add guessed geometry: first populate the measurement/evidence checklist in the
[enclosure plan](../docs/hardware/enclosure-plan.md) from the actual FRDM
board, Waveshare DSI panel and J15 cable, PIR, USB-C PD cabling, and thermal
tests (ADR 0008; the mounting direction is the VHB-bonded bathroom-mirror plate
with modular sensor bays in the enclosure plan).

The first mechanical prototype should be a low-detail fit and cable-routing
test. Production-looking bezel and wall/desk mounting work begins only after
the electronics and wake/display paths are stable.
